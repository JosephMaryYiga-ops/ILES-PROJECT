ILES - Internship Logging & Evaluation System
User Manual

1. Getting Started

Accessing the System
Live URL: https://iles-frontend.onrender.com
Supported browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge

User Roles

Student - Submits weekly logs, views feedback, checks evaluation
Workplace Supervisor - Reviews logs, provides scores and comments
Academic Supervisor - Monitors progress, final evaluations
Administrator - Manages users, assigns supervisors, sets evaluation criteria

2. Student Guide

2.1 Submitting a Weekly Log
Step 1: Login with your student credentials
Step 2: Click Submit Log in the navigation bar
Step 3: Fill in week number and log content
Step 4: Select placement if applicable
Step 5: Click Submit Log

2.2 Viewing Your Logs
Step 1: Click My Logs in the navigation bar
Step 2: View all submissions with status
Status meanings:
- Draft: Not yet submitted
- Submitted: Pending review
- Reviewed: Feedback given
- Approved: Final approval

2.3 Viewing Your Evaluation
Step 1: Click My Evaluation in the navigation bar
Step 2: View overall score and breakdown by criteria

3. Supervisor Guide

3.1 Reviewing Logs
Step 1: Login with supervisor credentials
Step 2: Click Review Logs in the navigation bar
Step 3: Select a log to review
Step 4: Enter feedback/comments and score (0-10)
Step 5: Click Submit Review

4. Administrator Guide

4.1 Managing Users
Step 1: Login as Administrator
Step 2: Click Users in the navigation bar
Step 3: To add user: Click + Add New User
Step 4: To delete user: Click Delete next to user
Step 5: To assign supervisor: Click Assign Supervisor next to student

4.2 Setting Evaluation Criteria
Step 1: Click Criteria in the navigation bar
Step 2: Click + Add Criteria
Step 3: Fill in criteria name, weight percentage, evaluator type
Step 4: Click Create Criteria

Solution: Check username/password, ensure account is active

Solution: Check all required fields are filled
